Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I book?

Use the booking form above or message us on WhatsApp at 0322 2134 222 with your game, day, time and number of players. The team confirms your slot in the chat.

What are the rates?

Rates depend on the game and your group size. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you the current rate card.

Can I book the whole place?

Yes. For birthdays, tournaments and team outings, send us your date and headcount and we'll put a package together.

Where exactly are you?

We're in Karachi. Tap "Send me the location" and we'll drop the map pin straight into your WhatsApp.

Do I need my own cue or controller?

No. Cues, chalk, controllers and PC setups are all ready for you. Bring your own if you've got a favourite.
